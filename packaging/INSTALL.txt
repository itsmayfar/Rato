RATO by MAYFAR - installation (macOS 11 or newer, Apple Silicon and Intel)

1. Double-click "RATO-Installer.pkg".
   If macOS says it cannot check the installer for malicious software:
   open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway",
   then open the installer again. (RATO is not yet signed with an Apple Developer ID.)
2. Follow the installer. It installs:
     /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/RATO.vst3
     /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/RATO.component   (Audio Unit)
     /Applications/RATO.app                              (standalone, for quick testing)
3. FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > "Find installed plugins" (or "Find more plugins"),
   then tick the star next to RATO so it shows up in the Plugin database.
   Add it to a channel like any other instrument: Channel rack > + > RATO.

User presets you save are stored in ~/Music/RATO Presets.
